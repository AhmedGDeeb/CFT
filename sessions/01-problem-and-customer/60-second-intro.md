# 60-Second Introduction

**Delivered:** Session 1, Fri 6 Nov 2026 (revised for SentinelMesh pivot)
**Length:** ~60 seconds

Hello, I'm Ahmad Deeb.

The climate problem I'm working on is wildfire detection in Syria. The country lost twelve thousand hectares of natural forest in 2025 alone, and fires are often detected only when someone sees the smoke — in remote areas where there is no reliable phone coverage and no grid power. By the time a report reaches responders, the fire is already too big to contain easily.

My solution is SentinelMesh — a solar-powered, self-organising mesh network built on low-cost ESP32 microcontrollers. Edge nodes with cameras run lightweight AI on-device to detect smoke and fire, then relay alerts node-to-node through the mesh, with no cellular or internet coverage needed in the field. The network runs on solar power with zero emissions, self-heals when a node fails, and can connect to the internet at a backbone gateway for dashboard access.

My target customer is forest protection agencies and environmental authorities in Syria — the bodies responsible for detecting and responding to these fires.

Thank you.

## Delivery notes

- **Structure:** Problem → Why it persists → Solution → Customer
- **Key line:** *"By the time a report reaches responders, the fire is already too big to contain easily."* This is the sentence that makes the jury feel the urgency. Slow down for it.
- **The statistics:** "12,000 hectares" and "2025" are from Global Forest Watch . If asked, you can add that Syria lost 40% of its 2000 tree cover between 2001 and 2025, and fire was the single largest driver.
- **Likely follow-up:** *"Who exactly is your customer, and have you spoken to any yet?"* Your answer: the Ministry of Emergency and Disaster Management and the General Organization for Remote Sensing's FIRMO platform are the institutional buyers . UNDP Syria issued a tender for an integrated early detection system in November 2025, which confirms the procurement signal . Say you are currently scheduling interviews.
- **One caution:** the introduction says "my target customer is forest protection agencies" — be ready to name one specifically if asked. The Ministry is the safest answer, since it publicly announced it is installing early-detection sensors and has only completed 20% of that project .
