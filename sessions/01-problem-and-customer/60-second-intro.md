# 60-Second Introduction

**Delivered:** Session 1, Fri 6 Nov 2026
**Length:** ~60 seconds

Hello, I'm Ahmad Deeb.

The climate problem I'm working on is water waste in agriculture. Smallholder farmers in rural Damascus decide when to irrigate without reliable local soil moisture and weather data. Monitoring stations are too sparse and too expensive, and forecast models are weakest exactly where water stress is highest. The result is over-irrigation, wasted freshwater, wasted pumping energy, and avoidable crop loss.

My solution is a low-power ESP32 sensor network — part of the Internet of Things. Nodes collect soil moisture, temperature and humidity, and communicate through a self-organising mesh network, so no internet or cellular coverage is needed in the field. The network runs on solar power with zero emissions. Lightweight AI runs directly on the devices to filter noise and decide when irrigation is actually needed. And the mesh can expand and connect to the normal internet at the backend, through a gateway on the backbone.

My target customer is smallholder farmers in rural Damascus.

Thank you.
